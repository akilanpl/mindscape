native interpretation, actual original graph inference, concept/action influence,
no-network/no-optimizer inference, unrepaired verification, bounded memory,
training authorization refusal, safe checkpoint replay, language intent/span
forward/backward, matched baseline shapes/counts, and historical preservation.

attributed. Arbitrary graduate/research prose is unsupported and receives an
availability response. No benchmark victory or learned language competence is
inferred from random engineering diagnostics.
