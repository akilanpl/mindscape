# Research hypotheses

Explicit state-transition learning may improve data efficiency, OOD performance
and evidence-supported output in bounded domains. This is untested. Deterministic
algorithm performance alone cannot establish learned generalization or superiority.

Compare answer-only supervision (A), trajectory supervision (B), and concept plus
limited examples plus environment feedback (C). C must not silently receive full
trajectory supervision. Equalize backbones, test sets, examples, decoding, epochs
and compute where practical; explicitly report additional procedural knowledge.
