# V5 structured transition prototype

The mechanism generates state effects, rather than ranking V4 candidate proposals. Domain encoders map public entities into 24 latent units. Two rounds of an action-conditioned message function process directed relationships; a shared effect update changes each entity embedding. Domain heads predict uncorrected numeric state deltas; a shared precondition head predicts action validity. No reference validity masks or correct deltas constrain the output. The same core parameters receive source/target gradients in shared fits.

The proposed abstraction is a computational dependency graph: public entities, directed relationships, a proposed action, independently observed effects. It does not equate a digit with an AST node or physical object. Domain features and heads preserve semantics. Three relation channels are domain-local roles (register source/write, AST operand/reverse edge, physical adjacency/displacement); their common latent treatment is a hypothesis to test. Entity-order equivariance and action sensitivity are checked on native MPS. Untrained native engineering checks have no scientific-success interpretation.

Microdomains: register add/sub updates; changing a literal in a small arithmetic AST with real WASI subtree outputs; one-step impulses under pinned Pymunk with fully observed parameters. Composition holds out a third register, a second AST layer, and a second physical object. Physics has no collision or spring interaction in this bounded pilot. AST internal before-values are masked and zeroed at inference; leaves, operators and operand edges are public. Hidden values are rejected unless masked/zeroed. No claim of hidden-parameter learning is made.

Complete frozen protocol and manifest: `configs/v5/protocol.json`, `configs/v5/tasks.json`. There are 44 distinct tasks/domain (24 train, 8 qualification, 12 composition); optimizer target supervision is the first 12 train tasks. Three optimizer seeds repeat task sets. Source auxiliaries use their 24 train tasks. Every learned condition has the same architecture and initialization; domain-only trains its core on target data only, shared transfers genuine source gradients, randomized permutes source effects/validity within domain. Every fit has 120 updates ×16 graphs; costs differ with domain/node dimensions and are recorded. There are 27 fits; no model-budget escalation or repeat search.

Qualification uses valid/invalid actions and changed executable effects, plus fixed persistence headroom. It reads no composition targets for gate selection. After qualification passes, composition labels are generated and hash-bound separately; they never enter optimizer batches. Procedural expert execution is perfect by construction in these microdomains and must be reported. Qualification does not claim headroom above that expert, or prove shared learnability.

Primary scientific endpoint is coding composition normalized MSE: shared must beat domain-only and randomized controls by at least 10% mean, with positive gains on every seed. It must also beat persistence by 20%, reach .70 validity balanced accuracy on every shared seed, remain finite, and not worsen other domains versus domain-only by over 10% mean. All thresholds precede training. Effects, preconditions, integer/invalid no-op and physics kinematic consistency, task correctness, training curves, compute, source hashes, weights and optimizer receipts are recorded. Successful completion alone cannot support rule-induction or transfer claims.

Stop on DATA_NO_GO before training or NO_GO after one pilot. Passing permits separately frozen confirmation only. This is experimental bounded transition learning, not a Qwen rebuild, full repair/planning system, or general intelligence claim. V3/V4 source and evidence are untouched.
