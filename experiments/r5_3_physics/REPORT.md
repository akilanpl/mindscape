# R5.3 physics — implementation and frozen campaign

Status: NOT_RUN. No new neural superiority claim or trained checkpoint exists. Original V3/V3.1 NO_GO evidence is unchanged.

V3's concept/memory rollout was catastrophically unstable under motion OOD and undercovered uncertainty (36.8% motion,47.9% interaction). Matched and approximate contact/spring controls often outperformed it. V3.1 bounded kinematics improved engineering but its inference imported training and cumulative marginal variance ignored position–velocity covariance; diagnostic widening was not evidence of learned uncertainty superiority. Existing V3.1 motion-memory and interaction-concepts final160 checkpoints (seeds11/23, best archived development-stress32-step seed-mean MSE) are retained as frozen references with corrected kinematic covariance, not retrained matched controls. Historical scales/results are not compared directly with the new panel.

R5.3 uses four-substep public integration, bounded object-relative learned radial/dissipative pair forces with equal/opposite internal forces, public approximate floor/circle collision priors and known indexed spring topology. The matched model has identical parameters/supervision/budget but learns bounded node accelerations. This supplied procedural scaffold is not learned discovery of physics. Floor/contact priors can be wrong; they are predictors, never verifier repairs. Conservation is scoped to internal forces with known masses, not external forces, hidden-mass mismatch, or dissipative contacts. Rotations, arbitrary shapes and real-world regimes are untested.

Public request `mindscape.physics.transition/5.3.1` admits only four observations,masks,future hypothetical actions,and known contexts. Hidden material/mass inputs must be zero; priors persist through each trajectory. Missing velocities are estimated from observations. Training alone reads future target states; the transitive inference imports exclude training,simulator,dataset and verification code. No private scene object or target enters prediction.

Uncertainty uses32 seeded particles, initial sensor/velocity uncertainty,persistent hidden-parameter priors,and learned velocity innovations. Nonlinear contacts are propagated through whole trajectories. Saved full12x12 covariance retains position/velocity and cross-object covariance. Terminal expected squared-distance risk uses the same predicted mean/covariance. Marginal95% intervals receive coverage AND interval-score gates; widening alone earns no credit. Tail quantiles are coarse with32 particles; hidden priors intentionally do not know shifted simulator parameters. No evaluation-based calibration.

Fresh protocol:48 train,12 development,24 IID,28 structural OOD scenes. Distinct split seeds and identities; historical sealed files are not accessed. Seven OOD strata per family,2 independent scenes/stratum. Development is frozen but unused for selection or calibration. Horizons8/16/32/64,32 primary. Six nativeMPS fits (structured/matched × seeds11/23/47),160 updates each,batch16,960 updates total. Training is4-step free-running MSE plus innovation NLL with final-checkpoint-only selection. Legacy frozen candidates,analytical contacts/springs,analytical free-flight,constant velocity and persistence receive identical public requests.

Training-only qualification requires48 independent training scenes,20 contact frames,8 spring scenes and mean analytical baselineMSE≥0.0001. It never filters scenes. Failure stops DATA_NO_GO; engineering/integrity failures preserve FAILED_CAMPAIGN. No retries. Before optimization native checks require finite multistep gradients,MPS placement,internal momentum and permutation consistency without indexed springs.

Scientific gates apply separately to motion/interaction: OOD MSE≤80% of strongest control; IID≤105%; velocityMSE no worse;95%coverage85–99%; interval score no worse;contact accuracy≥80%;mean floor penetration≤0.05;64-stepMSE≤100;planning success≥10pp above strongest control. Frames,horizons and repeated seeds do not enlarge independent-scene denominators. Point metrics by family/split/horizon/stratum and individual-seed rows remain archived.

Archive restoration checks every byte. No new evaluation result exists yet; native execution is required for a scientific verdict.
